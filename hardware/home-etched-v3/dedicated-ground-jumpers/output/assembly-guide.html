Three dedicated GND jumpers

Fit all three insulated wires on the component side, then solder underneath. Each joins pad 1 to pad 2 of the same G-number. No wire shares a module/ESC header hole; no middle solder tap. Six new holes: 1.2 mm drill, 3.2 mm copper lands. Match the holes in the diagrams, not nearby sensor/header pins.

The short new copper track from ESC3 GND ends at G3.1; jumper G3 connects it to common GND at G3.2. G2 does the same for the ESC4/right ESP ground group. G1 joins the shared BMP280/GY-271 return. Use insulated wire where it crosses other copper. G1 passes beneath a raised sensor module: fit the wire before the module and keep it below the assumed 9 mm module PCB height.

All fourteen module/header GND endpoints are common only after all three wires are fitted. Native DRC/parity and the independent assembled-net graph pass; these are CAD checks, not a physical continuity test. With power disconnected, check every GND against J1/lidar GND, then check 5 V and 3.3 V for shorts.

MPU6050 AD0 is intentionally grounded to select address 0x68. It is an address input, not another sensor GND supply pin. BMP280 SDO is also intentionally grounded (address 0x76).

Power: only J4/ESC3 pin 2 supplies the carrier from a verified regulated 5 V BEC; the other BEC positives remain isolated. Disconnect that positive lead before USB powering. Battery/motor current stays on a separate PDB. Exact module clone fit, paper scale, supply and interfaces remain untested. Firmware and disabled motor outputs are unchanged.

Use this revision's three-copy 1:1 mask at Actual size / 100%, with no Fit to page or extra mirror. The PDF contains no text. Old masks lack these six holes and must not be used for this revision.
