Lidar gap + common GND

The old lidar power jumper is removed. Fit the TWO insulated ground wires below before powering. They reuse existing soldered header pins: no extra drilled holes. G2 has a middle stripped/soldered tap; it is one continuous wire, with three connection points. Native CAD checks pass; physical fit, print scale, BEC and real interfaces remain untested.

Use insulated wire, e.g. AWG22 for the short common return. Solder to the exposed ends of existing header pins on the underside; do not try to push another wire into a filled hole. Strip only the solder points. Keep insulation intact where wires cross copper. G1's SDO attachment is already a PCB GND strap for BMP address 0x76; it is not a connection to SDA.

Power: only J4/ESC3 BEC pin 2 supplies FC_5V. Measure a regulated 5 V first; the other three BEC outputs are isolated. Disconnect that BEC positive lead before USB powering. Motor battery/current paths stay on a separate PDB. Logic and sensor VCC are 3.3 V.

Before etching: paper-fit the actual modules and verify 22.86 mm ESP row spacing and 2.54 mm module pin pitch. The BMP280 is rotated relative to the other sensors; match pin 1/VCC, not just board colour. Before power, fit G1/G2 and use a multimeter to confirm every GND and check 5V/3V3 for shorts.

Firmware: MPU SDA9/SCL8 matches the current bench. This PCB's other pins are a prepared map; the bench does not drive motors and has not been changed or flashed. See pin-audit.md before adapting future FC code.
