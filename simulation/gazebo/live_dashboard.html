Waiting for controller…

After enabling manual flight, gently move the left stick up to take off. Centre it to hold height. Right stick commands angles, not angular rates. A held tilt accelerates sideways; centring levels the drone but does not brake horizontal drift. Gentle uses a squared stick curve and 5° maximum tilt; normal allows 35°. Use small opposite tilt to brake. RT boosts, LT reduces. Down+LT held two seconds disarms.
