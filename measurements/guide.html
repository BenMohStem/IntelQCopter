For rotating-prop measurements use a rigid rated stand, remote operation and suitable protection. Do not hold a powered motor by hand. Verify mechanical bandwidth: a fast ADC does not make a slow scale fast.

Basic calculations

For NN repeated readings y1,…,yNy_1,\ldots,y_N:

yˉ=1N∑k=1Nyk,s=∑k=1N(yk−yˉ)2N−1,uA(yˉ)=sN.\bar{y}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{k=1}^{N} y_k,\qquad s=\sqrt{\frac{\sum_{k=1}^{N}(y_k-\bar{y})^2}{N-1}},\qquad u_A(\bar{y})=\frac{s}{\sqrt{N}}.

Here yˉ\bar{y} is the mean, ss is the sample standard deviation, and uA(yˉ)u_A(\bar{y}) is the standard uncertainty of the mean from repeatability. The last formula assumes independent samples. Filtering often creates correlation. A fixed assembly has a fixed mass; repeated scale readings fluctuate. Their standard deviation does not include every calibration bias.

For separate independent uncertainties, combine their variances. For example:

uc=uA2+ureference2+uresolution2.u_c=\sqrt{u_A^2+u_{\mathrm{reference}}^2+u_{\mathrm{resolution}}^2}.

Avoid counting the same contribution twice. For correlated inputs:

u2(f)≃∇fTΣ∇f.u^2(f)\simeq \nabla f^T\Sigma\nabla f.

For resolution step qq, a uniform rounding model gives

uresolution=q12.u_{\mathrm{resolution}}=\frac{q}{\sqrt{12}}.

Use that equation only when the uniform model is justified. A quoted ± limit alone does not establish a Gaussian standard deviation. See the full mathematical guide for correlated samples, Allan variance, apparatus procedures, propagation and worked examples.

Session notes

Record date, apparatus photos, raw file hash, software commit, units, instrument model/calibration, temperature, battery ID/voltage, exact motor/prop model, ESC settings, sample frequency, filtering, axis/sign convention, mounting and rejected samples. Reference measurements also have uncertainty; record it instead of calling them exact truth. Measure the final flying assembly again after component changes.

These procedures and empty CSV headers were carried forward from the RP2350 project. They contain no measured flight data; the same identification methods apply to the ESP32 competition airframe.
