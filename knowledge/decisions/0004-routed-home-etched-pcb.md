# D-0004 - user requests complete PCB design

Date: 2026-10-26. Supersedes the user-designs-PCB-only scope in D-0002/D-0003;
their module identities, connectivity and uncertainty remain applicable.
Latest instruction: "make the pcb for me".

Provide a separate 90 x 90 mm bottom-copper carrier with wide tracks, large pads,
solid GND, insulated wire links and three actual-size, text-free toner masks.
Full original logical connectivity is retained. W links are schematic components,
so native DRC cannot silently skip a wire. Raised sockets are required because
low insulated links pass below modules; socket courtyards and full body envelopes
describe different heights. USB access was revised using the official drawing.
No physical fit, etched-board continuity, flight, BEC current or firmware acceptance
is inferred from CAD results. No firmware or arming changes. Exact clones and F450
mounting pattern remain unmeasured. Preserve original projects and failed trials.
